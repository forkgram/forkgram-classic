# Forkgram Classic

[<img src="https://fdroid.gitlab.io/artwork/badge/get-it-on.png" alt="Get it on F-Droid" height="80">](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.forkgram.classic/)

A fork of Telegram for Android that preserves the **classic interface** — the calm, flat, readable look from before the "Liquid Glass" era.

As mainstream messengers drift toward translucent, glassy and ever-more-animated UIs, Forkgram Classic freezes the familiar Telegram design in place and keeps it that way.

It is based on [Forkgram](https://github.com/Forkgram/TelegramAndroid) and inherits its quality-of-life tweaks.

## Privacy

Forkgram Classic adds no telemetry, no analytics, no crash-reporting, and no third-party network endpoints on top of upstream Telegram for Android. All traffic goes only to Telegram's official servers.
